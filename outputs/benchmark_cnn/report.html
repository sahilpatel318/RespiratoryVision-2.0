Limitations

Small synthetic stress grid, no human recordings. Error is conditional on prediction availability. Bootstrap intervals describe this generated mixture, not clinical uncertainty. Inspect matched_fusion_subset in summary.json for a like-for-like comparison. Timing includes shared enhanced extraction for each method and is not additive.
