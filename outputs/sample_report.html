Methodology

Streaming grayscale ROI, Farneback flow, optional robust background translation subtraction, active-quadrant aggregation, displacement integration, detrending and Butterworth band-pass filtering. Welch, autocorrelation and cycle-interval estimates are compared before provisional quality gating. Spectral share is not probability.

Failures and limitations

Synthetic validation does not establish performance on people. Translation compensation cannot resolve rotation, parallax or independent body movement. Geometric ROI requires appropriate framing; automatic anatomy detection is unavailable. A single rate cannot characterize pauses or changing respiration. Thresholds are provisional and uncalibrated. Inspect exclusions, rejected cycles and background tracking coverage.
